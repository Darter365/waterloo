**Artillery.** Each infantry division has a foot battery of six 6-pounders and two howitzers (Durutte's has four guns and two howitzers). Each cavalry division has a horse battery. I and VI Corps each have a reserve battery of six 12-pounders and two howitzers. The Guard brings 96 guns at Waterloo; its campaign total was 126. Batteries are pooled into the grand battery when you order one.

**Absent:** Girard's 7th Division (left behind after Ligny) and Teste's 21st Division (with Grouchy).

The Guard figures are for the morning of 16 June. About 350 Guard infantry were lost at Ligny, mostly from the Young Guard. The game's Guard has 23 infantry battalions, matching the number historians agree on.

**Artillery.** About 156 guns, mostly 9-pounders and 6-pounders, plus Royal Horse Artillery troops (Mercer's, Ramsay's, Bull's howitzers, and Whinyates's rockets).

**At Hal and Tubize, not on the field (about 17,000):** Prince Frederik's 1st Netherlands Division and the Indies Brigade, Colville's other two brigades, and two Hanoverian hussar regiments. This is the force that can march in when the west flank is threatened.

**Strongpoints at the start**

- **Hougoumont:** 1/2nd Nassau (about 860), about 300 Hanoverian riflemen and jäger, and the light companies of the Coldstream and 3rd Guards, all under Macdonell.
- **La Haye Sainte:** Baring's 2nd Light Battalion KGL, 390 men with Baker rifles.
- **Papelotte and its neighbours:** Prince Bernhard's Nassauers.

## Sources and gaps

- **Main source:** [Wikipedia, Waterloo campaign order of battle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterloo_campaign_order_of_battle), which I read in full. Its French Guard infantry figures come from Sokolov's archival study; its other Guard units from Pigeard. The Anglo-allied present-at-Waterloo totals come from that page too. The research brief gives the context and cross-checks.
- **Gap: starting positions.** Taken from the usual accounts of the battle and not yet checked against a period map. Step 4 does that.
- **Gap: I and II Prussian Corps on the day.** Brigade strengths after Ligny aren't in the source. The 60–80% figure is my estimate.
- **Gap: French II Corps after Quatre Bras.** The game deducts about 4,000 across its brigades, spread in proportion to their size *(est.)*.
- **Gap: artillery.** Battery guns are as listed in the source, but how the grand battery was actually put together is not, so you assemble it with orders in the game.
